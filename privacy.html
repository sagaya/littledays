How Little Days handles your data

Everything you log in Little Days (feeds, sleep, diapers, growth, notes, photos and other records) is stored on your device. The Service Provider does not run servers for Little Days and cannot see your records.

  • iCloud sync and family sharing. If you are signed in to iCloud, your records sync through your own private iCloud database, operated by Apple. When you invite family members or caregivers, the records you share are visible to the people you invite. You can stop sharing at any time in the app.
  • Apple Health. Only if you allow it, the app reads and saves caregiver weight records in Apple Health. This data stays in Apple Health on your device and is not sent to the Service Provider.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Only when you tap Start recording, your voice is transcribed on your device so you can review a care note before saving it. Recordings are not uploaded to the Service Provider.
  • Camera and photos. Photos you take for supplies are stored with your records on your device and, if enabled, in your iCloud.
  • Weather. To show local weather on the Home screen, the app looks up the main city of your device's time zone and requests the weather for it from Apple WeatherKit. It does not use GPS or your precise location. You can turn weather off in Settings.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Timer updates shown on your other devices are delivered through Apple's push notification service.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. Little Days contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software and never sells or shares your data.
  • Deleting your data. You can delete individual records in the app, archive or export them, or delete the app to remove data from your device. Data synced to iCloud can be removed in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Little Days is a logging tool for parents and caregivers. It does not provide medical advice.
