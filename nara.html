Moving from Nara Baby

You can take your history with you. Nara Baby lets anyone export their records, with or without a subscription, and Little Days can import that file. It takes about a minute per child.

1. Export from Nara Baby

  1. Open Nara Baby and go to the main Activity screen.
  2. Tap the avatar next to your child’s name.
  3. Tap Export Data.
  4. In the share sheet, choose Save to Files. The file is a CSV named like export_narababy_name_date.csv. Don’t open or edit it in a spreadsheet first.
  5. If you track more than one child in Nara, repeat this for each child.

These steps come from Nara Baby’s FAQ. If the export itself doesn’t work, Nara’s team can help at help@narababy.com.

2. Import into Little Days

  1. Install Little Days. It’s free, with no subscription, no ads and no account.
  2. On first launch choose Import from another app, or later go to Settings › Import and export › Import data.
  3. Choose Nara Baby and select the CSV you saved.
  4. Check the preview. You’ll see how many records will be added and which child they belong to. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

3. Bring your family along

Invite a partner, grandparent or nanny from Settings › Family & caregivers. Sharing works through iCloud and is free for everyone you invite.

What comes across

Breastfeeding, bottle feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, growth, vaccines, temperatures and medications, with the notes attached to each record. Any other activity type is skipped, and the import preview lists exactly what was skipped and why before you confirm.
